A quick guide to referrals that may suit mobile physiotherapy input.

Neurological and progressive conditions

Stroke, MND, ABI, TBI, spinal cord injury, Parkinsonism, MS and changing neurological function.

Complex disability and high-support needs

Support needs where routines, positioning, equipment, safety and care-team input overlap.

Mobility, transfers and falls risk

Walking, transfer safety, mobility aids, access barriers and falls-risk concerns.

Respiratory decline, fatigue or carer training needs

Changing capacity, pacing, respiratory considerations or support-worker education needs.

Why mobile

Why assess function at home?

A clinic assessment may not show the barriers in a person’s usual environment.

Clinical approach

Transfers at home

Review bed, chair and toilet transfers in the space and with the supports actually used.

Equipment and access

Check how mobility aids, positioning and access barriers affect everyday movement.

Fatigue and routines

Assess pacing and activity tolerance around the person’s usual tasks.

Care-team carryover

Share practical strategies, training and documentation with relevant people when consent is in place.

DVA, private and Support at Home

DVA and private referrals are accepted. Support at Home enquiries are welcome where services can be arranged through the participant’s registered provider, including under an applicable self-management arrangement.

Tandem is not a registered Support at Home provider. Read FAQs for details.

NDIS physiotherapy

Self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants only. Tandem is not an NDIS-registered provider.

iCare / LTCS physiotherapy

iCare/LTCS enquiries are welcome, with commencement subject to relevant scheme approval where required. NSW workers compensation referrals are subject to applicable insurer/scheme requirements.
