Mobile neurological and complex disability physiotherapy across Western Sydney

Tandem Physio provides home and community-based physiotherapy for people with neurological, respiratory, mobility and complex disability-related needs. Suitable referrals may include NDIS, iCare/LTCS, DVA, Home Care Package, workers compensation and private clients.

Service pathways

How Tandem can support you

Three practical pathways depending on the person's current goals, risks and care-team needs.

Assessment and recommendations

For clients needing functional review, therapy planning, equipment-related input, care-team guidance or documentation.

Ongoing therapy and capacity building

For clients needing regular rehabilitation, mobility practice, fatigue management, respiratory monitoring, functional progression or carer/support-worker training.

Review, training and documentation

For care teams needing updated strategies, support-worker education, function-linked reports, risk clarification or practical recommendations.
