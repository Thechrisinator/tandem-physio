Who we help

Tandem Physio supports adults with neurological, respiratory, mobility and complex disability-related needs, particularly where therapy needs to be coordinated with family, support workers, case managers or funding bodies.

  • People living with stroke, MND, spinal cord injury, traumatic or acquired brain injury, progressive neurological conditions or complex chronic pain.
  • Clients who require home-based therapy, mobility review, transfer practice, equipment recommendations or carer training.
  • Families and referrers needing clear recommendations, funding justification and practical next steps.

Practical rehabilitation in the right environment

For many clients with disability or neurological conditions, the most useful assessment occurs at home or in the community. This allows therapy to consider real-world mobility, transfers, fatigue, access barriers, routines, equipment, support worker needs and safety risks.

Where appropriate, recommendations are documented clearly so that families, support coordinators, case managers and treating teams can understand the clinical reasoning behind the proposed supports.

How referrals work

A simple pathway from enquiry to assessment, therapy planning and ongoing support.

1

Submit enquiry

Send the referral form, call or email with the client’s location, funding type and broad reason for referral.

2

Confirm suitability

We confirm service area, funding pathway, goals, urgency and whether Tandem Physio is the right fit.

3

Initial assessment

Assessment occurs at home or in the community, with consent-based involvement of family, support staff or case managers.

4

Plan and document

Recommendations, therapy goals, risk considerations and reporting needs are clarified after assessment.

5

Ongoing support

Therapy, reviews, carer training, care-team liaison or discharge planning are arranged as clinically appropriate.

Resources and FAQs

Useful information for clients, families, support coordinators and case managers.

Typical initial assessment

Initial assessments usually take 45–120 minutes depending on complexity. The session may include history, goals, mobility, transfers, respiratory considerations, equipment, routines and support needs.

Funding and claiming

We accept NDIS self-managed and plan-managed participants, LTCS/iCare, DVA, Home Care Packages and private clients. Medicare referrals may be accepted where eligible; a gap payment may apply.
