Visits in the Parramatta area

Tandem Physio considers home and community visits in Parramatta, Westmead, Northmead and Winston Hills, alongside other suburbs in the service-area directory.

Parramatta marks the eastern end of the broader service area, which extends towards Penrith in the west, Glenorie in the north and Liverpool in the south. Travel to edge locations is confirmed for the exact address.

A listed suburb does not guarantee availability. Visits depend on clinical suitability, funding, travel feasibility and current capacity.

Returning home or reviewing an ongoing need?

Enquiries can relate to rehabilitation after discharge or an existing difficulty that has changed. Explain the task the person wants to work on and the support currently available.

A diagnosis or discharge date alone does not determine whether a home visit is suitable.

Continuity of rehabilitation

Connect a rehabilitation plan with life at home

After a hospital or rehabilitation stay, everyday tasks may involve different furniture, distances and assistance from those used during inpatient therapy. Home assessment can consider how the existing recommendations fit those circumstances when community care is appropriate.

Relevant discharge recommendations, precautions, equipment and current assistance needs help inform planning. With consent, communication with the treating team can support continuity rather than asking the person or family to reconstruct the plan alone.

Read about stroke physiotherapy and acquired and traumatic brain injury physiotherapy for more on rehabilitation goals. Medical management remains with the treating medical team.

Reviewing function beyond discharge

Ongoing physiotherapy needs may include walking, transfers, mobility aids, fatigue, positioning or changes in the support required at home. The assessment starts with the person’s current priorities and whether the existing approach still fits.

For example, a referral might describe difficulty reaching the bathroom, reduced confidence moving through the home or a family member needing clearer guidance about assistance. These practical details help explain why a review is being requested.

See the full clinical focus areas, including multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s and Parkinsonism and falls risk. Learn how mobile assessment considers equipment, routines and support.

Help plan the first appointment

  • Explain the broad reason for referral and the person’s suburb.
  • Confirm consent or appropriate authority to refer.
  • Identify relevant discharge recommendations or current care-team contacts.
  • Discuss access, timing and who usually assists at home.

Start with a suitability enquiry before sending detailed information. Use the referral form when ready and share only information relevant to the referral.

Referrals, funding and availability

Private and self-funded enquiries are welcome. NDIS physiotherapy is for self-managed and plan-managed participants only; Tandem is not an NDIS-registered provider.

For iCare and Lifetime Care, relevant scheme approval is required where applicable. Other pathways and their conditions are explained in the funding FAQs. Funding, required approvals and availability are confirmed before services start.
