Best suited referrals

Case managers often need a quick sense of whether a provider is the right fit. This section is designed to answer that quickly.

  • Clients with severe injury or complex disability who need assessment and treatment in their home environment.
  • Clients requiring mobility review, transfer practice, falls-risk management or physical management planning.
  • Clients where support worker training, routines or manual handling guidance are relevant.
  • Clients whose presentation involves respiratory considerations, changing physical capacity or a broader MDT context.
  • Referrals where practical recommendations and clear follow-up matter.

Referral and commencement pathway

1.Referral received and suitability discussed.
2.Funding / approval pathway confirmed where required.
3.Initial assessment arranged in the home or community.
4.Goals, recommendations and ongoing plan clarified.
5.Therapy, liaison and reporting provided as clinically appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

These interactive FAQ sections make the page feel less static.

Do you accept LTCS / iCare referrals?

Yes. Tandem Physio can support suitable iCare / LTCS referrals where the service fit, approval pathway and clinical needs align.

Do you also see workers compensation clients?

Yes, suitable workers compensation referrals may also be accepted. The exact pathway depends on the scheme, approval and clinical requirements.

What types of sessions are usually most useful?

Initial home-based assessment, mobility and transfer review, physical management planning, support worker education and ongoing clinically guided therapy are common.

How can case managers refer?

Use the referral page, send an email enquiry, or call to discuss the referral, location, funding pathway and clinical goals.
