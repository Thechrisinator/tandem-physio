iCare, LTCS and severe injury physiotherapy

Mobile physiotherapy for severe injury and complex neurological presentations, with clear communication for case managers, insurers, families and treating teams.

Clinical presentations

Tandem Physio is suited to clients requiring home-based assessment, functional rehabilitation, equipment-linked therapy, carer training or ongoing review after severe injury.

  • Traumatic brain injury or acquired brain injury
  • Spinal cord injury and complex mobility needs
  • Severe orthopaedic injury with functional limitation
  • Neurological disability, respiratory risk or progressive functional decline
  • Manual handling, transfer practice, carer training and equipment-related rehabilitation

Approval before service

For iCare, LTCS, Workers Care, CTP or workers compensation referrals, Tandem Physio will confirm the scheme, claim details, approved service type, approved hours, rate, travel, report writing and invoicing pathway before commencing billable services.

For ordinary workers compensation referrals, SIRA requirements and approval processes apply.

What to include in the referral

The right information at the start helps avoid delays in approval, booking and billing.

Scheme and claim details

Confirm whether the referral is LTCS, Workers Care, workers compensation, CTP Care or another pathway, and include claim or participant details if available.

Approval scope

Provide approved physiotherapy hours, rate, travel, reporting, non-face-to-face time and relevant approval dates where known.

Clinical reason

Outline the current functional issue: mobility, transfers, falls risk, carer training, respiratory concerns, equipment or decline.

Care team

Include case manager, family contact, support coordinator, GP, OT or other clinicians where consent is in place.

Reporting needs

Note any required progress report, therapy request, funding review, case conference or insurer-specific documentation.

Location and access

Include suburb, parking/access requirements, support worker presence and any known safety considerations.

For case managers and insurers

Tandem Physio aims to make severe injury referrals administratively clear: written approval first, transparent billing, practical functional goals and communication with the wider care team where consent is in place.
