Information that helps before booking

These details help prevent delays and make the referral easier to triage.

Referral details

  • Client suburb and preferred contact person.
  • Scheme type: LTCS/iCare, workers compensation, CTP or other pathway.
  • Claim or participant number if available.
  • Reason for referral, urgency and key risks.
  • Known approval requirements, report needs or case-manager preferences.

Clinical priorities

  • Mobility, transfers, manual handling and falls risk.
  • Physical management, positioning, tone or contracture risk.
  • Respiratory considerations, fatigue and escalation planning.
  • Support-worker education and home program implementation.
  • Clear updates for the broader care team.

iCare / severe injury FAQs

Useful information for case managers, insurers and referrers.

Do you accept LTCS / iCare referrals?

Yes. Tandem Physio can support suitable iCare / LTCS referrals where the service fit, approval pathway and clinical needs align.

Do you also see workers compensation clients?

Yes, suitable workers compensation referrals may also be accepted. The exact process depends on the scheme, approval status, billing pathway and clinical requirements.

What should be confirmed before treatment starts?

For insurer-funded referrals, the scheme, claim details, approved hours, rate, travel/reporting approval and billing instructions should be clarified before billable treatment commences.

Can case managers refer directly?

Yes. Case managers can use the referral page, email referral details, or call to discuss suitability, location, urgency and funding pathway.
