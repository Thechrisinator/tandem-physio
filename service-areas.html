Availability

How service area is checked

Service availability is considered alongside the clinical reason for referral, funding pathway, travel distance, timing and current capacity.

A listed suburb does not guarantee availability, and an unlisted suburb is not automatically excluded. The safest next step is to provide the suburb and broad referral reason so fit can be reviewed.

Helpful details

  • Client suburb and usual visit location.
  • Funding pathway and referral reason.
  • Whether home, supported accommodation or community visits are needed.
  • Any access, timing, risk or support-worker considerations.
