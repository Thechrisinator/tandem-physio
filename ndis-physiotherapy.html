When physiotherapy may be helpful

These are common reasons support coordinators, participants and families refer to Tandem Physio.

⇄

Mobility and transfers

Changes in walking, transfers, falls risk, wheelchair mobility, bed mobility or functional safety at home.

⚠

Functional decline or increased risk

Reduced endurance, changing physical capacity, contracture risk, pain, fatigue or increased reliance on informal and paid supports.

🧩

Coordination and planning

Need for carer education, equipment input, practical therapy planning or clear documentation to support the wider team.

How Tandem Physio helps

A more practical and visually engaging overview of the work you commonly do.

Complex disability and neurological rehabilitation

Therapy aimed at improving safety, function and day-to-day participation in the person’s usual environment.

  • Mobility, balance and falls-risk review
  • Transfer practice and manual handling education
  • Positioning, contracture risk and physical management
  • Practical home-based rehabilitation

Respiratory and high-support presentations

Support for participants where respiratory considerations, fatigue, equipment and carer training influence function and safety.

  • Respiratory monitoring and education
  • Cough assist, suction and airway-clearance related support
  • Coordination with the broader treating team
  • Escalation planning where clinically appropriate

What referrers can expect

Designed to feel a little more polished and useful for support coordinators and families.

  • Clear communication about suitability, service area and funding pathway.
  • Assessment in the home or community where functional issues can be seen directly.
  • Practical treatment and education aligned with client goals, risks and support needs.
  • Clinically reasoned recommendations and reports where required.
  • Communication with the MDT when consent is in place.

Frequently asked questions

Simple expandable sections add some functionality without making the page feel cluttered.

Do you see NDIS participants at home?

Yes. Home and community visits are central to the service, particularly where real-world assessment of transfers, mobility, fatigue, access and support needs is important.

Which NDIS clients are the best fit?

Tandem Physio is best suited to adults with neurological, respiratory or complex disability-related physiotherapy needs, especially where support workers, equipment or clinical documentation are part of the picture.

Can you provide reports or written recommendations?

Yes, where clinically appropriate. Reports and letters can help communicate function, risk, equipment considerations and therapy recommendations to the participant and broader team.

How do I refer?

You can use the online referral page, email an enquiry, or call directly to discuss whether the referral is the right fit.

Need to discuss an NDIS referral?

Use the online referral page or contact Tandem Physio directly to discuss availability and suitability.
