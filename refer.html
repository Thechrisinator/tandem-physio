Refer a client

Participants, families, support coordinators, case managers and healthcare professionals can use the same PracSuite referral form.

We’ll review your referral and contact you to discuss the next steps. We aim to respond within 1 business day.

Before sharing information

Provide only information needed for the referral. If referring someone else, you must be authorised and have their consent where required; we may verify authority.

Read the referral collection notice and Privacy Policy, and make the notice available to the person you’re referring.

Submitting a referral does not guarantee acceptance or an appointment.

If the form does not load or you prefer a separate tab, open the PracSuite referral form (new tab).

Not sure if Tandem Physio is suitable?

Contact Chris to discuss your needs before submitting a referral.
