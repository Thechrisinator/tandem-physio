Refer a client

Use the referral form below for self-managed or plan-managed NDIS, iCare/LTCS, NSW workers compensation, DVA, Support at Home or private physiotherapy enquiries across Western Sydney and The Hills District. Tandem is not an NDIS-registered provider. Support at Home enquiries are welcome where services can be arranged through the participant’s registered provider, including under an applicable self-management arrangement; Tandem is not a registered Support at Home provider. iCare/LTCS service commencement is subject to relevant scheme approval where required. NSW workers compensation referrals are subject to applicable insurer/scheme requirements.

Before submitting

You must be authorised to provide this referral information and, where required, have the participant’s consent to share it with Tandem Physio. We may verify authority before proceeding.

  • Include the suburb and funding pathway.
  • Briefly describe the functional concern.
  • Avoid unnecessary sensitive information initially.

Check suitability before referring

Not sure whether Tandem is the right fit? Participants, families and professionals can check suitability before making a formal referral.

For an initial enquiry, share only:

  • The suburb or general location.
  • The funding pathway, if known.
  • A brief functional reason for the enquiry, such as help with mobility or daily activities.
  • Your role in the enquiry, such as participant, family member or coordinator.
  • Your preferred return contact.

You do not need to provide the participant’s full name, diagnosis, medical history or reports. Avoid unnecessary participant-identifying or sensitive clinical information. No formal referral is needed to check suitability.

Call 0410 048 142 or email tandemphysionsw@gmail.com. Please keep email enquiries brief; email is not a secure clinical-record system.

Chris can discuss likely service fit and the next step with you. A suitability enquiry does not confirm clinical suitability, acceptance or an appointment.

Preparing a formal referral

The same referral form can be used by participants, families, coordinators, case managers and treating teams. Choose the pathway closest to the person making the referral.

Participant or family member

Who this is for

People seeking support for themselves, a family member or someone they help coordinate day to day.

Helpful information

Suburb, funding pathway, main mobility or daily function concern, preferred contact person and any urgent safety issue.

Usually clarified next

Service area, consent, availability, goals and whether mobile physiotherapy is likely to be suitable.

Continue to referral form

Support coordinator or case manager

Who this is for

Coordinators and case managers making a formal referral for physiotherapy, reporting or therapy support.

Helpful information

Funding type, suburb, broad referral reason, urgency, known risks, consent status and whether reports or training may be needed.

Usually clarified next

Referral suitability, communication preferences, approval requirements and the most useful first assessment focus.

Continue to referral form

Provider or treating professional

Who this is for

GPs, therapists, nurses, providers or treating teams seeking physiotherapy input for a shared client.

Helpful information

Functional issue, relevant precautions, diagnosis context, current supports, equipment, key risks and consent to liaise.

Usually clarified next

Role boundaries, communication needs, documentation requirements and whether the referral matches the mobile service scope.
