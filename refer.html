Before submitting

Please confirm the client or authorised representative has consented to the referral and relevant information sharing.

  • Include the suburb and funding pathway.
  • Briefly describe the functional concern.
  • Avoid unnecessary sensitive information initially.

Useful referral details

  • Client suburb and preferred contact person.
  • Funding type: NDIS, iCare/LTCS, workers compensation, DVA, HCP or private.
  • Main reason for referral.
  • Current mobility, transfer, falls, respiratory or functional concern.
  • Known risks, urgency or access issues.
  • Whether a report, therapy block or training is required.
