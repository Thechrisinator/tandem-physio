Before referring

Please provide enough information to confirm suitability, location, funding pathway and urgency. Detailed clinical documents can be shared later once consent and service arrangements are confirmed.

  • Client suburb and broad reason for referral
  • Funding type: NDIS, LTCS/iCare, workers compensation/SIRA, DVA, HCP or private
  • Referrer contact details and role
  • Urgency or key safety concerns
  • Consent from the client or authorised representative for the referral

Privacy note: Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information at the first enquiry stage. Tandem Physio will request further documentation only where clinically relevant and consented.

What happens after referral

A clear process from initial enquiry through to assessment and therapy planning.

1

Referral received

We review location, funding type, urgency and clinical fit.

2

Suitability confirmed

We clarify goals, risks, availability and consent requirements.

3

Funding checked

For iCare, workers compensation or funded schemes, written approval may be required before services commence.

4

Assessment booked

Assessment is completed at home or in the community where function can be observed directly.

5

Plan documented

Recommendations are communicated to the client, family and care team where consent is in place.
