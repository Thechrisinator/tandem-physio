MND physiotherapy · Western Sydney and The Hills

Mobile physiotherapy for people living with motor neurone disease

Tandem Physio provides home and community-based physiotherapy for adults living with motor neurone disease (MND) across Western Sydney and The Hills District.

Support as needs change

Physiotherapy shaped around daily life

Physiotherapy focuses on supporting mobility, comfort, participation and safer everyday routines. Goals are guided by what matters to the person, their current abilities and the support available at home.

As MND progresses, the plan may shift from maintaining activity to adapting transfers, positioning, equipment and carer support. Review is guided by changes in function, fatigue and priorities, rather than a fixed exercise programme.

Care is provided by Chris Burgess, Tandem’s Principal Physiotherapist. Read About Chris for his background and clinical approach.

When to consider a referral

  • A new diagnosis and a need to plan practical support.
  • Changes in walking, balance, transfers or falls risk.
  • Fatigue making everyday movement more difficult.
  • A need to review mobility aids, positioning or carer routines.

Assessment at home

Practical support in the usual environment

Home visits allow assessment of the spaces, tasks, equipment and support routines used day to day.

Mobile physiotherapy

Mobility and transfers

Review walking, balance, falls risk and movement between beds, chairs and other everyday surfaces, including the assistance needed.

Individualised exercise and pacing

Tailor movement, range-of-motion work and activity to current capacity and goals. Exercise is adjusted to avoid overexertion, excessive fatigue and pain, with review as needs change.

Positioning and comfort

Consider movement, joint range and supported positions that may improve comfort and make everyday care easier, alongside the wider team’s advice.

Mobility aids and equipment

Assess and trial mobility aids where appropriate, and contribute physiotherapy advice on transfers, standing and positioning. Complex seating, assistive technology (AT) and home-modification needs are addressed collaboratively with occupational therapists and relevant specialists.

Carer and support-worker education

Provide practical guidance on agreed mobility, transfer, positioning and exercise strategies, taking account of the person’s preferences and the support available.

Review and documentation

Review changes in function and support needs, and provide clinically appropriate recommendations linked to everyday tasks, risks and goals.

Multidisciplinary care

Working alongside the existing care team

With consent, Tandem can liaise with the person’s neurologist or MND clinic, GP, occupational therapist, speech pathologist, respiratory team, MND advisor, support coordinator and other treating clinicians.

Sharing functional changes and practical recommendations helps the team plan ahead and coordinate support. Physiotherapy complements ongoing medical and specialist MND care.

Coordinating specialist input

Breathing or cough concerns are discussed with the treating medical or respiratory team. Any respiratory physiotherapy input depends on clinical suitability, clinician competence and the agreed care plan; specialist assessment and ventilation decisions remain with the relevant team.

Swallowing concerns require speech pathology and medical input. Physiotherapy observations can inform equipment and access planning, with home-modification assessment and design led by appropriately qualified professionals.

Referrals and service area

Arrange MND physiotherapy in Western Sydney

Enquiries are welcome from people living with MND, families and carers, MND clinics, GPs, allied health professionals, support coordinators and case managers. Referrals on another person’s behalf require their consent or appropriate authority.

Home visits are considered across Western Sydney and The Hills District, including Blacktown, Parramatta, Castle Hill, Kellyville, Liverpool and Fairfield. Check Service Areas for details; location, clinical fit and current availability need to be confirmed.

For NDIS physiotherapy, Tandem accepts self-managed and plan-managed participants only and is not an NDIS-registered provider. Private enquiries are also welcome. Funding arrangements and any required approvals are confirmed before services commence.

Useful starting information

  • Suburb, preferred contact and funding pathway.
  • Main functional concern and any recent change.
  • Current mobility, transfer support and key risks.
  • Relevant treating team and consent to communicate.

Share only information needed to assess or progress the referral. An enquiry or referral does not guarantee an appointment.
