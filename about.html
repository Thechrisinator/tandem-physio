About Tandem Physio

Clinician-led mobile physiotherapy for complex referrals

Tandem Physio is led by Chris Burgess, Principal Physiotherapist, with a focus on neurological disability, complex rehabilitation, functional change and practical support for clients with higher support needs.

About Chris Burgess

Chris Burgess is the Principal Physiotherapist at Tandem Physio. His clinical work focuses on neurological disability, complex rehabilitation, functional change, mobility, respiratory considerations and practical support for clients with higher support needs.

Tandem Physio was developed as a clinician-led mobile physiotherapy service for referrals where function is best understood in the person's usual environment. This may include the home, supported accommodation, community settings or other everyday environments where mobility, transfers, fatigue, routines and support needs can be observed directly.

Clinical focus

A practical home and community approach

Assessment and therapy planning focus on function, safety, risk, goals and the person's support context.

Neurological and complex disability focus

Support for referrals involving neurological disability, progressive conditions, complex rehabilitation, mobility change and higher support needs.

Function in context

Home and community assessment can clarify how mobility, transfers, fatigue, equipment and routines work in real daily environments.

Communication and documentation

Where clinically appropriate and consent is in place, recommendations can be shared with families, support coordinators, case managers and treating teams.

Practice values

How Tandem Physio works

A steady, clinically cautious approach that keeps recommendations linked to the person's real needs and environment.

Practical

Therapy is linked to real tasks, routines, environments and goals.

Clinically reasoned

Recommendations are based on functional presentation, risk, goals and the person's support context.

Collaborative

Communication with families, coordinators, case managers and treating teams occurs when consent is in place.

Respectful

Care is delivered with attention to dignity, autonomy, privacy and the person's preferences.

Referral and communication approach

Helpful referrals include location, funding pathway, consent status, the main functional concern and any known risks. Tandem Physio will usually clarify suitability, service area, availability and the most useful first assessment focus before commencing care.
